How to read this

vs EMA compares spot alpha price to the chain's own moving price — the metric root stakers are paid against, so a large discount or premium is the cleanest dislocation signal on a subnet.

Flow τ/d is SubnetEmaTaoFlow scaled to a day: positive means TAO is being staked into the subnet faster than it leaves.

Emis/mcap annualises the subnet's TAO emission against its alpha market cap — high values mean the market is paying little for a large share of rewards, which is a value screen and also a dilution warning.
