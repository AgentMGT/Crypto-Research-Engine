    How the simulator works
    • Prices come from the latest site refresh (three times a day, or when you press Scan now). Between refreshes, prices and your positions' values stay fixed.
    • Fills use each subnet's pool: buying x τ from a pool with T τ and A alpha gets you A × x ÷ (T + x) alpha. Your own orders move the pool until the next refresh, so splitting an order doesn't dodge slippage. An assumed fee of 0.05% is charged on every swap.
    • Plan checks compare every order with the plan as it stood at that refresh: setup, position limits, pool-depth cap, basket cap, tranches. You can place an off-plan order, but the journal marks it, and your plan adherence score counts it.
    • Not simulated: staking emissions and validator take. Positions only gain or lose from price.
    • Your account lives in this browser. Clearing site data erases it, and it doesn't sync between devices. Use Export to keep a copy and Import to restore it or move it to another device.
