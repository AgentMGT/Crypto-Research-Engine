A bot that trades four practice accounts on its own, with simulated money only. Each time the site refreshes, it follows fixed rules: trend and oversold setups for crypto, your trading plan for Bittensor subnets, your altcoin plan for the ALTCOINS scanner's picks, and the pre-listing system for coins on their own DEX pools. When the AI reviewer is switched on, Claude checks every new entry and can skip it, giving a reason. Stops, targets and plan exits always go through. The bot never connects to an exchange or a wallet.
