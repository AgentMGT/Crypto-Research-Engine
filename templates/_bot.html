A bot that trades two practice accounts on its own, with simulated money only. Each time the site refreshes, it follows fixed rules: trend and oversold setups for crypto, and your trading plan for Bittensor subnets. When the AI reviewer is switched on, Claude checks every new entry and can skip it, giving a reason. Stops, targets and plan exits always go through. The bot never connects to an exchange or a wallet.

The bot's rules {% set c = bot_cfg.crypto %}{% set t = bot_cfg.tao %}
  • When it trades: on each refresh, at most once every {{ bot_cfg.min_hours_between_ticks }} hours. Extra Scan now runs within that window don't trade.
  • Crypto account starts with ${{ "{:,}".format(c.start_usd) }}. It only looks at coins ranked in the top {{ c.max_rank }} with at least ${{ "{:,}".format((c.min_volume_usd / 1e6)|int) }}M of daily volume. It holds at most {{ c.max_positions }} positions and opens at most {{ c.max_new_per_tick }} new ones per refresh. Positions are long only, and fills include a {{ c.fee_pct }}% fee plus slippage scaled to volume.
      {% for k, s in c.setups.items() %}
    • {{ s.label }}: {{ s.size_pct }}% of the account per trade, stop-loss at -{{ s.stop_pct }}%, target at +{{ s.target_pct }}%, and it sells after {{ s.max_hold_days }} days if neither is hit. {% if k == "trend" %}Entry needs a signal score of at least {{ s.min_score }} and one of the tags {{ s.tags_any|join(", ") }}. It skips coins tagged {{ s.tags_none|join(" or ") }}.{% else %}Entry needs a 4-hour RSI at or below {{ s.max_rsi }} on a top-{{ s.max_rank }} coin.{% endif %} Regimes: {{ s.regimes|join(", ") }}.
      • {% endfor %}
    • It also sells when a coin it holds gets tagged {{ c.exit_tags|join(" or ") }}. After selling a coin, it waits {{ c.cooldown_hours }} hours before buying it again.
    • Stops and targets are checked against 4-hour prices since the last run, so a brief wick inside 4 hours can be missed. If the price gapped through a stop, the fill is at the worse price.
  • Bittensor account starts with {{ t.start_tao }} τ and follows the trading plan to the letter. It buys plan entries one tranche at a time, at most {{ t.max_new_per_tick }} per refresh, within the plan's position, pool-depth and basket caps. It sells everything on a plan exit or the plan's stop, and trims a third on a trim signal (at most once every {{ t.retrim_days }} days). Fills use each subnet's pool with a {{ t.fee_pct }}% fee. Staking emissions aren't counted, so returns understate what holding earns.
  • AI reviewer: runs when the site has an Anthropic API key (the ANTHROPIC_API_KEY repository secret). Claude sees each proposed entry with its numbers, the regime, the bot's holdings and recent headlines, and answers take or skip with a reason and a 1–5 confidence. Without the key, the bot trades on the rules alone.
  • The rules live in data/bot.json in the repository. Change them there and push.
