    How the crypto simulator works
    • Prices are CoinGecko's aggregate USD price, refreshed every minute while the page is open.
    • Costs: a 0.10% taker fee on every fill, plus slippage of 10 × your order's share of the coin's 24-hour volume (so $10,000 of a coin trading $1M a day costs about 10%). Capped at 20%.
    • Shorts are 1× with no borrow or funding cost. The full size is set aside as collateral, and profit or loss is the price change.
    • Stops and targets trigger at your level, also while you're away: on each visit the simulator replays price history since your last visit and fills any stop or target that was crossed. If both were crossed in the same price bar, the stop is assumed to fill first.
    • Your account lives in this browser. Use Export to keep a copy and Import to restore it.
